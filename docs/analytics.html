At a glance

The essentials from the verified judgment catalog. Text totals combine the current production inventory with newly downloaded local sources.

Judgments by Year

Number of ECHR judgments delivered per calendar year since the Court's inception, illustrating the steady growth of the Strasbourg docket over six decades.

The final bar is a partial year — the corpus ends part-way through it (see the catalog cut-off above), so the last column counts only the months harvested so far. It is a cut-off, not a collapse in the Court’s output. How to read this

Judicial Collections Over Time

Annual output from the Grand Chamber, Chambers and three-judge Committees. Counts come from the official judicial collection, not inference from case titles.

The final year is partial through the catalog cut-off shown above.

Violation Rate by Year (%)

Share of judgment records with at least one tagged violation, by calendar year. Includes judgments without a tagged merits finding in the denominator; it is not an application success rate or a measure of state compliance.

The final year is partial and rests on far fewer cases, so its rate is noisier than the rest of the series. How to read this

Outcomes by Year

Annual breakdown of case outcomes — violation only, non-violation only, mixed findings, and no finding — showing how the Court's caseload composition has evolved over time.

The final year is partial (the corpus ends part-way through it — see the catalog cut-off above); read the last column as an incomplete sample, not a trend. How to read this

Outcomes by Article

Compare main Convention and Protocol articles, not combinations or subparagraphs. The violation share counts judgments with at least one violation tag, out of judgments with a violation or non-violation tag for that article.

Exact counts for this view
Each judgment counts once per article. Mixed means both outcome tags for different complaints or aspects.
ArticleViolation shareWith outcomeViolation onlyMixedNon-violation onlyNo outcome tag

Example: 6-1 and 6-3-d count once under Article 6; P1-1 remains Protocol 1, Article 1. Linked tags such as 14+8 contribute to both article families, not necessarily two standalone findings. Mixed judgments appear in the violation share, but are shown separately. No outcome tag does not mean no violation. English/French text versions are not counted twice.

This is a descriptive metadata comparison, not the probability of winning a claim. HUDOC tag conventions.

Coverage, unresolved matches and methodology

This is a metadata-based ranking, not our own full-text citation count (the extracted application numbers are HUDOC's, without citation context). Counts are lower bounds from resolved references; missing or ambiguous references can affect ordering. Application number alone is insufficient when several judgments are possible. Unresolved references are not guessed. Citing and cited documents must both be in the judgment catalog; cited decisions are excluded.

Judgments by State

Judgment records by respondent state. Counts describe the selected judgment catalog, not all applications, admissibility decisions or population-adjusted litigation rates.

Counts use the complete verified judgment catalog, including records whose available source text is French. A multi-state judgment contributes once to each respondent state, so state totals can exceed the judgment count.

Violation Rate by Country

Proportion of cases resulting in at least one violation finding per respondent state (top 25 by caseload, minimum 10 cases). Normalises raw case counts to reveal which states have the highest per-case breach rates.

Rates use the verified English-flagged HUDOC metadata catalog, including judgments whose inventoried source text is French. Language versions are not counted twice; see the catalog scope and language limits at the top.

State Outcome Table

Breakdown of outcomes (Violation only, Non-violation only, Mixed, No tagged finding) and violation rate for each respondent state with at least 5 cases. Top 20 shown — sorted by total caseload.

Distribution of HUDOC thesaurus keywords across all judgments. These controlled-vocabulary tags are assigned by the Court's registry and capture the legal themes, Convention articles, and procedural aspects of each case (see measured field coverage below).

Annual frequency of the five most common thesaurus topics, tracking how the Court's docket composition has shifted across core Convention rights over the decades.

Articles by Respondent State

Top respondent states in cases involving a given Convention article — select an article to see which countries are most litigated under that provision, with total case counts and violation counts side by side.

Precedent Concentration

Cumulative share of the ten most frequent reference strings in populated HUDOC citation metadata. This is string concentration, not resolved precedent influence.

Denominator: all reference strings in populated HUDOC citation metadata, not all judgments or citations in their texts. How to read this

Paragraph Distribution

Nonempty text rows by parser/index section, combining inventoried production text and local English/French source bundles. Header and applicant-table rows are included; this is not a count of numbered judicial paragraphs.

French text is included. Section assignment in newly parsed bundles has not yet undergone source-exact QA. Applicant tables can dominate Appendix counts; compare structural rows cautiously.

Coverage & Source Languages

Measured availability, not an accuracy score. A blank optional field does not mean a judgment contains no discussion of that subject.

Language of inventoried judgment texts

An English metadata flag does not prove an English source text exists. Older production texts have no confirmed source-language field, so they remain unknown rather than being guessed as English. French bundles are original texts, not translations generated by this tool.
